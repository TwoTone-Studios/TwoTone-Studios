About the client: Beavertown

Beavertown is one of the best known craft breweries in the UK, famous for bold artwork and a loyal community of fans. Merchandise is part of how that community shows up.

The brief

Beavertown needed a partner who could deliver custom brewery t shirts to a consistent standard:

  • Black t shirts that match the brand artwork exactly
  • A big back print plus a left chest print on every shirt
  • A huge volume delivered to a consistent standard

The right print method matters at this scale. See DTF vs screen printing.

How we made it

  1. Sourcing. We sourced heavyweight black cotton tees across the full size range, chosen because they hold seven colours of ink cleanly without cracking.
  2. Artwork. The design was separated into seven colours, with a screen made for each and registration checked so the layers line up exactly.
  3. Sampling. A first print on the exact garment was approved before the main run began.
  4. Production. Shirts were printed in batches with the back print and the left chest print, cured for wash durability and checked against the approved sample.
  5. Finishing. Every shirt was counted, folded and packed by size, ready for delivery.

Finished product

We produced 70,000 black t shirts using screen printing. Each shirt carries a seven colour back print and a left chest print, giving the artwork the depth and punch that screen print does best, and every shirt matches the last.

The outcome

Screen printing is the best value at this scale, and seven colours on black needed careful set up and colour matching. The result is a run of custom brewery t shirts that looks as good on shirt 70,000 as on shirt one. We have a great working relationship with the Beavertown team.

How screen printing works

Each colour in your design gets its own fine mesh screen. Ink is pulled across the screen onto the garment, then cured so it lasts. Once screens are made, printing is fast, which is why screen printed t-shirts in volume are so cost effective.

When screen printing is the best choice

  • You need 50 or more of one design.
  • Your artwork has one to three solid colours.
  • You want a classic, long lasting finish.
  • You are ordering bulk t-shirts on a budget.

Frequently asked questions

Is screen printing cheaper than DTF?

On runs of 50 or more with one to three colours, yes. For small and full colour orders, DTF is usually cheaper.

How many colours can you screen print?

Several, but each adds a screen and cost. One or two is cheapest.

Is screen printing durable?

Yes, it is hard wearing and made for repeated washing.

How does it work?

Pick a garment, upload your artwork and place it in our studio. You see a live mockup straight away. Happy? Check out. We print it, check it and send it to you.

How long does it take?

Most orders are printed and dispatched in 3 to 5 working days. Bulk and embroidery orders can take a little longer, and we tell you before you pay.

How much is delivery?

Standard UK delivery is shown at checkout before you pay. Group orders can ship to one address or to each person.

What file should I upload?

A PNG or vector file (SVG, PDF, AI) with a transparent background works best. Aim for at least 300 dpi at print size. Not sure? Upload what you have and we will tell you.

Can I order just one?

Yes. We are a small business too, so there is no minimum: one tee, one hoodie, one cap. Prices drop the more of the same garment you order, and for bigger team orders we prepare a tailored quote.

Can I have an invoice?

Yes. Ask us and we will send a business invoice for any order, with your company name on it.

What if I do not like it?

You approve a proof before anything is printed. If we get something wrong, we fix it or remake it.

Do you do bulk and workwear?

Yes. Teams, venues and brands get trade pricing, repeat order reordering and private labelling. Tell us what you need and we will quote.
